Madison Farmers' Market

Check out Madison's iconic Farmers' Market on Capitol Square! The market is open every Saturday from April 11th to November 14th, 2026. Vendors that are a part of the market are producer-only. They sell produce, poultry, cheese, baked goods, flowers, and many more goodies! Come as early as 6:15am and stay as late as 1:45 pm. Be sure to check out the restaurants along the square and stop by the lakefront for breathtaking views!

What You Can Find at the Market

  • Produce
  • Poultry
  • Cheese
  • Baked goods
  • Flowers
